PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle information

ApnaAutoCare respects your privacy. This page describes general practices for handling information connected with our website. The exact practices depend on the features enabled, service providers used, and information you choose to submit.

01

Information We Collect

Depending on how the website is configured and used, information may include:

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, phone number, vehicle details, and message when you submit an enquiry or booking request.
  • Request details: the services you ask about, preferred appointment details, and other information you choose to include.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, or usage information that may be processed by hosting providers or website tools.

We should collect only information that is relevant to the purpose for which it is requested. Do not submit passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information through a general enquiry form.

02

How We Use Information

Where applicable, submitted information may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and service enquiries.
  • Review and manage booking requests.
  • Communicate about a requested service or appointment.
  • Maintain website functionality, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or other permission required by applicable law.

03

Enquiries and Bookings

If you submit a booking request or contact form, the information you enter may be processed by the form provider and by the people responsible for handling that request.

A submitted request does not necessarily mean an appointment is confirmed. Please check the booking instructions and any confirmation you receive.

If ApnaAutoCare uses Google Forms or another external form service, that provider may process information under its own privacy terms. Review the provider's policy before submitting information.

04

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website itself may use browser storage or similar technologies if features are added that require them. Hosting services, embedded content, analytics tools, or other third-party features may also use cookies or related technologies.

The technologies actually used should be identified before this policy is published. You can review your browser settings to manage cookies, although blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

05

Sharing Information

Personal information should only be shared where necessary for a stated purpose, with appropriate service providers, with your permission where required, or when disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law.

Depending on the actual website setup, providers may include hosting companies, form platforms, or technical support services. Confirm the providers in use before relying on this description.

This policy does not make a blanket claim that no third party ever processes information; embedded tools and infrastructure may process data according to their own terms.

06

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, loss, or disclosure.

No website or method of online transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The actual safeguards depend on the hosting, form services, access controls, and other systems in use.

07

Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry, manage relevant records, fulfil the stated purpose, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods should be established for each form, booking system, and service provider used by the website. Delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed, where appropriate.

08

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the handling of your personal data.

You can choose what information to include in an enquiry, subject to the information necessary to respond. You may also be able to manage cookies through your browser settings.

To make a privacy-related request, use the Contact page. Requests may need to be verified and are subject to applicable legal requirements.

09

Third-Party Websites and Links

Our website may link to external websites or display content from third-party services. Those services have their own policies and practices, which we do not control.

Review the relevant provider's privacy policy before sharing personal information on an external website.

10

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for people seeking information about vehicle care and related services. It is not designed to intentionally collect children's personal information.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information inappropriately, please contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

11

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its features, service providers, or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version should be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Review this page periodically to understand the policy currently in effect.

12

Contact Us About Privacy

If you have a question about this policy or want to make a privacy-related request, contact us using the website's contact page.
